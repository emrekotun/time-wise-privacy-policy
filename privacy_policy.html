Privacy Policy for TimeWise

Last Updated: December 6, 2024

Introduction

TimeWise ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the TimeWise mobile application.

Data Collection

TimeWise does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All information you enter into TimeWise (time entries, tags, notes, achievements, etc.) is stored locally on your device and never leaves your device.

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports
  • Device information
  • Contact information
  • Any other personally identifiable information

Local Storage

TimeWise uses local device storage to save:

  • Your time tracking entries
  • Custom tags and categories
  • App settings and preferences
  • Achievement progress
  • Streak information

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

Internet Connection

TimeWise is designed to work 100% offline. The app does not require or use an internet connection for any of its features.

Third-Party Services

TimeWise does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Backup and Export

TimeWise provides optional backup and export features that allow you to save your data to files on your device. You have full control over these files and can choose to share them or keep them private.

Children's Privacy

TimeWise does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
